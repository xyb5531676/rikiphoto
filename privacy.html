Privacy Policy

How Riki Photos collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: January 2025

Overview

Riki Photos ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you. By using our website or booking our services, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, and any details you include when you submit an enquiry or booking request through our contact form or by email. We may also collect non-identifying technical information such as browser type and pages visited, used only to improve the website experience.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to respond to enquiries, arrange and deliver photography sessions, send relevant updates about your booking, and maintain our business records. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Photographs & Image Rights

All images produced by Riki Photos remain the copyright of Riki Photos unless otherwise agreed in writing. Photographs delivered to clients are licensed for personal use. We will always seek your permission before using any session images for portfolio, promotional, or social media purposes.

Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies necessary for basic functionality. We do not use cookies to track you across other websites. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to view the site.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. Enquiry details are stored securely and retained only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time. To make a request, please contact us using the details below.
